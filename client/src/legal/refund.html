This Cancellation & Refund Policy is part of the OneDash Terms of Service and applies to payments made to Bitslab Technologies ("we", "us") for OneDash plans, add-ons and NFC cards. By paying for the Service you agree to it.

1. How OneDash billing works

  • Plans and add-ons are prepaid for the period you choose. They do not renew automatically and we never charge you without you starting a payment.
  • When the period ends, the plan expires and paid features stop until you buy again. We send a reminder before expiry. Your data is kept so you can continue where you left off.
  • Because nothing renews automatically, there is no subscription to cancel: simply do not buy the next period.

2. 48-hour refund on your first purchase

If OneDash is not right for you, you can ask for a full refund of your first paid plan purchase within 48 hours of the payment time shown on your invoice.

  • It applies once per account and business, to the first purchase of a paid plan only.
  • If you used the Service heavily in those 48 hours (for example large broadcasts, high message volumes or significant AI credit use), we may deduct the cost of that usage from the refund. We will show you the calculation.
  • Requests made after 48 hours are not eligible, except as described in section 4.

3. What is not refundable

  • Any purchase after the first one: buying another period, or buying again after a plan expired
  • Upgrades, after the first 48 hours of the original purchase. When you upgrade, the unused value of your current plan is credited to the new plan at checkout instead.
  • The unused part of a period when you switch to the Free plan. Switching to Free takes effect immediately and ends the paid plan.
  • Add-ons and AI credit packs once activated or partly used, and message or contact top-ups once used
  • Meta's WhatsApp conversation or message charges. These are billed under your WhatsApp Business Account's arrangement with Meta and are not collected or refunded by us.
  • Payment gateway or bank charges on international payments, and currency conversion differences
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for breaching our Terms of Service or Meta's policies

4. When we refund outside the 48 hours

  • Duplicate or wrong charges, such as being charged twice for the same order: refunded in full once confirmed.
  • Payment taken but plan not activated, if we cannot activate it within 3 business days: refunded in full, or activated, as you prefer.
  • Serious service failure on our side that makes the Service unusable for more than 72 consecutive hours during a paid period (not caused by Meta, your network or your account settings): a pro-rata refund or free extension for the affected time.
  • A major paid feature removed during your paid period, as described in our Terms of Service.

5. NFC cards

  • You can cancel an NFC card order for a full refund until it moves to "processing". After that the card is made for you and the order cannot be cancelled.
  • If a card arrives damaged, defective or does not match your order, tell us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will replace it free of charge, or refund it if replacement is not possible.
  • Cards are not refundable for errors in details you supplied, a change of mind, or loss after delivery to the address you gave.

6. How to ask for a refund

  1. Open a support ticket from your dashboard in the Billing category, or email bitslabtech@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Include your registered email, the invoice or transaction ID, and the reason for the request.
  3. We reply within 2 business days to approve or explain our decision.

7. Refund timelines

  • Approved refunds are initiated within 3 business days and go back to the original payment method only.
  • Banks and gateways usually take 5 to 10 business days to show the money. For payments made by bank or UPI transfer, we refund to the account the payment came from.
  • When a refund is issued, the related plan or add-on ends immediately and a credit note is issued against the invoice.

8. Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact us before raising a dispute or chargeback with your bank so we can fix the problem quickly. While a chargeback is open we may suspend the account. Chargebacks raised for payments that were genuinely made and delivered may lead to termination of the account.

9. Payments to sellers on OneDash stores

Purchases made from a business's OneDash online store are between that business and its customer. The business sets and handles its own cancellation, return and refund terms. Bitslab does not receive those payments and cannot refund them.
