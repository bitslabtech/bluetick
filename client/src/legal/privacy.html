This Privacy Policy explains how Bitslab Technologies ("Bitslab", "we", "our" or "us"), the company behind OneDash, collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal data when you use OneDash: our website, web dashboard and the OneDash mobile app for Android and iOS (together, the "Service"). It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules under it, and the privacy requirements of Meta, Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Please read it with our Terms of Service and Cancellation & Refund Policy. By creating an account or using the Service you consent to the processing described here. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 9).

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • Your account data (you, your business and your team): Bitslab decides how and why it is processed, so we are the data fiduciary.
  • Your customers' data (the contacts, chats, calls, orders and leads you manage in OneDash): you are the data fiduciary, and we process it only on your instructions as your data processor. You are responsible for having a lawful basis, the required notices and WhatsApp opt-ins for every person you contact through OneDash. Your customers should contact you first about their data; we will help you respond.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account and billing

  • Name, business name, email address and phone number
  • Password (stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash, never in readable form)
  • Billing address, GSTIN or tax number (if you provide one), country and currency
  • Plan, add-ons, invoices and payment records (amount, date, gateway reference). Card, UPI and bank details are entered on the payment gateway's own page and are not stored by us. If you pay by bank or UPI transfer and upload a payment screenshot, we keep that screenshot as proof of payment.
  • Team members you invite: their name, email, role and permissions

2.2 Data from Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram)

When you connect your WhatsApp Business Account through Meta's Embedded Signup (Facebook Login), we receive and store on your behalf:

  • WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, business phone number and display name
  • Access tokens that let us call the WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta Marketing APIs for you
  • Business verification status, quality rating and messaging limit
  • Message template content and approval status
  • Webhook events from Meta: incoming messages, delivery and read receipts, template decisions, account alerts and incoming voice call events
  • If you connect Meta Ads or Click-to-WhatsApp analytics: ad account IDs, campaign names, budgets and performance figures; linked Facebook Page and Instagram account IDs

2.3 Messages, contacts and business content

  • Contact names, phone numbers, emails, tags, groups, custom fields, notes and opt-in / opt-out status
  • Message content you send and receive (text, captions, button replies), media files (images, video, audio, documents), templates, quick replies and broadcast campaigns with their recipients and results
  • Automation flows, forms and their responses, and lead details captured through your forms or the "Book a demo" page
  • Online store data: products, prices, stock, your customers' orders and delivery addresses, coupons. Payments for your store are taken by your payment gateway; we store only the reference.
  • Digital business cards (vCard): the profile you publish, enquiries and view/scan counts; NFC card orders with the delivery address you give us

2.4 WhatsApp voice calls (if enabled)

Customers can call your WhatsApp Business number and your team answers in the OneDash mobile app. For each call we store the caller's number and saved name, when it came in, was answered and ended, its duration and outcome (answered, missed, declined, refused outside business hours, failed), which team members were rung and who answered or declined, whether an automatic missed-call or closed message was sent, and the technical setup data Meta needs to connect the call. We do not record, store or listen to call audio. Audio travels between the answering phone and Meta's WhatsApp call servers.

2.5 The OneDash mobile app

  • Sign-in session: a login token kept in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android), removed when you log out.
  • Push notification tokens from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service and the Expo push service, with your platform (Android or iOS). Used only to notify you of new messages and calls; deleted when you log out or delete your account.
  • Microphone: used only during a WhatsApp call you choose to answer, and to record sound if you capture a video.
  • Camera, photos and files: used only when you take a photo or video, or pick a file to upload or send. We receive only what you select.
  • Bluetooth (Android): used only to play call audio through a connected headset or car kit.
  • Proximity sensor: turns the screen off at your ear during a call; readings stay on the phone.
  • Crash reports: the error message and technical trace, operating system and version, app version, the screen you were on and your user ID. They never include message content, contacts or call audio.

The app does not read your phone's address book, collect your location, use an advertising identifier, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2.6 Technical and usage data

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city, never GPS)
  • Browser, device type and operating system; login times and session activity; an activity log of important account actions (for security and audit)
  • Feature usage, message volumes and AI credit usage (for billing, plan limits and product improvement)
  • Error logs and crash reports from the website and the app

3. How we use data, and our legal basis

  • To provide the Service you signed up for (your consent and our contract with you): sending and receiving WhatsApp messages, running broadcasts and automations, ringing your team for calls, the online store, vCards, analytics and every other feature.
  • Billing: taking payments, applying plan limits, issuing GST invoices.
  • Notifications: OTPs, billing receipts, plan expiry reminders, WhatsApp quality alerts, push notifications in the app.
  • AI features you choose to use (reply drafts, message improvement, chatbot answers): the relevant text is sent to our AI provider to generate the result.
  • Safety and security: preventing fraud, spam and abuse, enforcing our Terms and Meta's policies, investigating complaints.
  • Fixing problems and improving the Service, using error reports and aggregated usage.
  • Legal obligations: tax and accounting records, responding to lawful requests from authorities, and the duties of an intermediary under the IT Rules, 2021.

We do not sell personal data, rent it, or use your customers' data or message content for advertising or to train AI models.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run OneDash, under contracts or terms that require them to protect it:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform, Facebook Login, Marketing API): messages, templates, calls and ads data. Meta processes this under its own terms and the Meta Privacy Policy. When you connect WhatsApp or Meta Ads you also agree to Meta's terms for that product, which we link at the point of connecting.
  • Payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, PhonePe, Stripe, depending on what you choose at checkout): payment amount, your name, email and phone.
  • Cloud hosting and storage providers (servers, databases, file storage such as Amazon S3 or Cloudflare R2, CDN and DDoS protection).
  • Google (Gemini API) for AI features you use, and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android push.
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) for iOS push, and Expo (push delivery service) for app notifications. Push services receive the push token and the notification itself, for example a customer's name or number and the text of a new message, or a missed-call alert.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection on sign-up and login.
  • Your own team: members you invite see workspace data according to the permissions you give them.
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order or a lawful government request, and to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Bitslab.
  • A buyer or successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, who must honour this Policy. We will tell you before your data is transferred.

5. Where data is processed

Some providers above (for example Meta, Google, Apple, Expo, Cloudflare, Stripe and cloud storage providers) may process data outside India. We transfer data only to countries not restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act and rely on the providers' own security and data protection commitments.

6. How long we keep data

  • While your account is active, we keep your workspace data (contacts, messages, media, campaigns, call history and so on) until you delete it or delete your account.
  • When a plan expires, your data is kept so you can renew; paid features stop until you do.
  • After account deletion, see section 7.
  • Payment and invoice records: 8 years, as required by Indian GST and tax law.
  • Crash and error reports: as long as needed to fix the problem.
  • Meta access tokens: deleted when you disconnect WhatsApp or delete your account.
  • Push tokens: deleted when you log out or delete your account.

7. Deleting your account

The account owner can delete the account from Profile → Delete account in the mobile app or Settings → Profile → Delete Account on the website, confirming with the account password. You can also email us (section 12).

  • 30-day grace period: deletion takes place 30 days after the request. You can cancel at any time in those 30 days from the same screen.
  • What is deleted: contacts, conversations and messages, media files, templates, broadcasts, automation flows, forms, call history, online store, vCards, connected WhatsApp and Meta credentials, push tokens and settings. Voice calling is switched off on your WhatsApp number and every team member loses access.
  • What we keep: your name and email address; payment and invoice records (8 years, for tax law); account activity and AI credit usage records needed for billing and audit; and message delivery records for 180 days. Delivery records hold only the recipient's phone number, campaign name, delivery status and times, with the message text removed. We keep them to handle complaints, abuse reports and legal requests, as expected by the IT Rules, 2021, and permanently delete them after 180 days.
  • Team members cannot delete the workspace; they can ask its owner.
  • Data already sent to Meta (for example delivered WhatsApp messages) stays on WhatsApp and with your customers, under Meta's policies.

8. Security

We protect data with HTTPS/TLS for all traffic, bcrypt password hashing, AES-256 encryption at rest for WhatsApp and Meta access tokens, role-based access for your team, rate limiting, bot protection, verification of Meta webhook signatures, restricted administrative access and regular backups. No system is completely secure; keep your password private and tell us immediately at the email in section 12 if you suspect unauthorised access. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law you can:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and with whom it was shared
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate data (most of it directly in your account settings)
  • Erase your data, by deleting your account (section 7) or asking us
  • Export your contacts and campaign data
  • Withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal stops future processing, but some features will stop working and it does not affect processing already done.
  • Revoke Meta access at any time in Meta Business Settings → Integrations / Business integrations. OneDash will then stop sending messages for you.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapable
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (section 12), and if unresolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India

We reply to requests within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first, and some data must be kept by law (section 6).

10. Cookies and local storage

The website uses essential cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, protect forms from cross-site attacks and remember preferences such as dark mode. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile may set a functional bot-protection cookie, and Meta's JavaScript SDK, loaded only for WhatsApp Embedded Signup, may set cookies under Meta's cookie policy. The mobile app does not use cookies; it keeps your session in secure storage and preferences on your phone.

11. Children

OneDash is a business service for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.
